King Alloy Metals manufactures premium forged components trusted by industries that demand strength, reliability, and performance. WHO WE ARE

Delivering Reliability Through Forging Excellence

Located in Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab, King Alloy Metals is a leading manufacturer of open die forged products serving diverse industrial sectors.

Our expertise spans forged shafts, forged rings, die forging blocks, squares, flats, and custom-engineered forged components designed to meet demanding industrial requirements.

Combining metallurgical knowledge, advanced manufacturing processes, and rigorous quality control, we deliver forged products that consistently meet performance, strength, and reliability expectations.
